Between the moment a car arrives and the moment it is photographed, there are usually two to three weeks of work that nobody sees.

## Fluids, filters, and the things that age

Every car receives fresh fluids appropriate to its history. Rubber components — bushes, hoses, wiper blades — are checked and replaced where they have aged, even if they have not failed yet.

## Paint correction, not paint

We correct paint rather than repaint it. Original paint, carefully brought back, is worth more than a perfect respray, and it keeps the car honest.

## The final drive

The last step is always a long drive. Not a test, but the kind of drive the next owner will take. Anything that is not right comes back to the workshop.

Only after that do we photograph the car and publish it.
