The collection is small on purpose. We would rather show six cars we can stand behind than sixty we cannot.

## The first filter is history

Before we look at a car, we read it. Service records, invoices, registration documents and previous listings tell us more than a first impression can. Gaps are not always a problem, but they need an explanation.

## Then the car itself

Paint depth is measured panel by panel. The underside is inspected on a lift. We drive the car cold and warm, in traffic and on open road. Anything we find is written down and shared with you — including the small things.

## Finally, a question of taste

Some cars are technically sound and still not right for us: an unhappy colour combination, modifications that fight the original design, a specification that nobody will want in ten years. We pass on those, and we would rather tell you why than pretend otherwise.

## What you receive

Every car in the collection comes with its inspection notes, full photography and a clear account of its history. If you would like a second opinion, we will arrange an independent inspection and hand over the car’s file without conditions.
