# Credits

## Demo imagery

The photographs in `src/assets/media/` are 3D renders made specifically for
this template. They were rendered with [three.js](https://threejs.org) in a
virtual studio: an architectural hall, polished concrete, softbox lighting
and a film grade.

They use two third-party 3D models:

- **Ferrari 458 Italia** by vicent091036 on Sketchfab, as distributed in the
  three.js examples (`examples/models/gltf/ferrari.glb`). Manufacturer badges
  were recoloured where possible.
- **Car Concept** from the Khronos Group
  [glTF-Sample-Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept),
  licensed CC-BY 4.0. It is based on a public-domain model by Unity Fan.
  Khronos logos were removed from the renders.

The demo imagery is for **demonstration only**. Vehicle manufacturers' names
and designs are trademarks of their owners. "Vessant" and "Oberhall" are
fictional. Replace all demo imagery with your own licensed photography before
using this template commercially.
